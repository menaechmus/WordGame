    What does it mean?

    ? stands for any letter. A pattern puts letters in their places (C?T is CAT, COT, CUT); letters make words in any order, each used once (RETAIN? finds RETAINS).

        How to play

        1. Make words. Take turns laying tiles from your rack to make words that read across or down. Each turn's tiles go in one row or one column, with no gaps, and must join tiles already on the board. The first word covers the centre star.
        2. Score them. Every word your tiles make counts, including the short words formed alongside. Each letter scores the number on its tile; a blank stands for any letter you choose and scores nothing.
        3. Premium squares. 2L and 3L double or triple the letter on them; 2W and 3W (and the star) double or triple the whole word. They count only on the turn a tile first covers them.
        4. All seven. Use every tile on your rack in one turn for 50 extra points.
        5. Or not. Instead of playing you can swap tiles for new ones (while the bag still holds seven) or pass.
        6. The end. When the bag is empty and someone uses their last tile, the game ends: everyone loses the value of the tiles they still hold, and the player who went out gains it all. Three rounds in a row without a score also end the game.

        Wi-Fi: everyone plays on their own phone. One person chooses Wi-Fi on the menu and taps Host a game; the others choose Wi-Fi, tap Join a game and enter the four letters on the host's screen, or open the link the host shares. The host's word list, hints and word check apply to everyone. A phone that drops out comes back by itself, and while it is away the host can pass for it.

        Tap a tile, then a square, or drag it there. Tap a tile you have laid to take it back, or lay another on top of it to swap them. Drag tiles along the rack to arrange them. Hint shows the best plays for your tiles, and every other play on request. The magnifier (and Words on the menu) checks a word, finds words by their start, end, letters or pattern, and lists every two- and three-letter word; "2 & 3 letters" opens that list straight away. Choose the word list on the menu: Wordnik + added, the first choice, comes closest to the official lists; ENABLE is older and smaller; YAWL is the biggest, with many rare words. The small number on each score is how many tiles that player holds. On a phone, Zoom (or a double-tap on an empty square) enlarges the board.

        With a keyboard: click a square and type the word. Click the square again to type downwards. Shift and a letter lays a blank as that letter. Backspace takes back the last letter, Enter plays and Escape takes your tiles back.
